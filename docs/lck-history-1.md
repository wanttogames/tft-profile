# LCK-HISTORY-1
Initial 2022–2027 archive. Six yearly snapshots and ten team lineages. 2022–2024 use spring-opening roster articles, 2025 preseason roster with DRX cup bot-lane supplement, 2026 reuses the final-game snapshot in LCK-STOVE-2, 2027 reuses the contract-confirmed records in LCK-FA-1 (not refreshed by this patch). Sources and historical team names are in src/data/lckHistory.ts. These are selected snapshots, not all registered players, match history, exact contract dates or overseas careers. Multiple recorded players in a role are preserved. Do not infer employment between snapshots. Player detail pages require at least two observed years, and all players appear in the directory. Update static sitemap routes whenever historical data changes. Existing APIs, DB and dependencies are unchanged.
